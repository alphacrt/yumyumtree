About Yum Yum Tree

A mandi house with a feel-good twist.

Yum Yum Tree is known across Hyderabad for juicy Mutton Mandi, Al-Faham Chicken Mandi and generous, shareable platters. Our new concept is simple: great food should make you feel good and look your best. Warm light, comfortable seating, photo-worthy plates and flavours that bring friends and family together.
